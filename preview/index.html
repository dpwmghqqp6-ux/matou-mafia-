MATOU MAFIA RULES

6 reels · 5 rows · 20 paylines. Wins pay anywhere on a line: 3 to 6 matching symbols side by side, starting on any reel. Bomb Wild substitutes for all symbols except Safe. Highest win per line is paid.

  • Base 1×. Hunt 3× cost makes Safes about 4.6× more frequent. Buy Bonus 120× (level 3). Super Bonus 155× (level 4). All wins pay in steps of 0.10× bet.
  • Max one Safe per reel (6 max). 3 Safes → level 3 choice. 4+ Safes → level 4 choice between HEIST and GOLD RUSH.
  • Winning symbols explode and new ones tumble down (cascades) until no more wins. Same in Gold Rush.
  • HEIST: mystery money sacks, hold & spin (3 respins at level 3, 4 at level 4). Values reveal one by one at the end.
  • GOLD RUSH: 8 spins (12 at level 4) with cascades. Winning cells turn gold. A Gold Bomb pays every gold cell as 1/2/3 ingots or an ingot chest. Level 3 clears gold after pay; level 4 keeps them.
  • After the choice, pick a door for a feature-specific boost.
  • RTP 96.50% on every mode. Max win 5,000× per round.
  • Each spin is independent. No jackpot, gamble, or cashout.
  • With 2 Safes on reels 1–5, the cat may plant a Safe on reel 6. This is part of the RTP.
